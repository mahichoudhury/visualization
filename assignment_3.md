Visualization 1 - https://open.toronto.ca/dataset/neighbourhood-crime-rates/

    > What software did you use to create your data visualization?
    Excel

    > Who is your intended audience? 
    Toronto Residents and Policy Makers
    
    > What information or message are you trying to convey with your visualization? 
    I am trying to convey the effectiveness of policing initiatives on the 10 highest crime areas from 2014, over the last 10-years.
    
    > What aspects of design did you consider when making your visualization? How did you apply them? With what elements of your plots? 
    I wanted the visuals to speak for themselves - Concise title that helps draw the conclusion effectively, plotted line graph thats connected so that you can follow the trend, individual colours per line so easy to follow 
    
    > How did you ensure that your data visualizations are reproducible? If the tool you used to make your data visualization is not reproducible, how will this impact your data visualization? 
    While the visuals are easily reproducible, the only fact that you need to know before you reproduce it is that the data was first sorted in decending order by 2014 (first year on graph), so that the crime trends can be appropriately plotted
    
    > How did you ensure that your data visualization is accessible?  
    Appropriate X and Y axis
    Distinguisable colour palette
    Horizantal shaded lines are various increments 
    
    > Who are the individuals and communities who might be impacted by your visualization?  
    Residents and community member of the plotted areas
    Local MPs of the respective aresa
    Local Police

    > How did you choose which features of your chosen dataset to include or exclude from your visualization? 
    I wanted to show a trend - and that requires multiples of a data (in this case years). I also wanted it to be easy to provide context and create narratives that can be easily replicated.
    
    > What ‘underwater labour’ contributed to your final data visualization product?
    Clean up data
    Sort Decending order by crime rate
    Clean up the graph
